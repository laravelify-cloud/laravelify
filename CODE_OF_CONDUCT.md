# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want Laravelify to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to servers, Laravel, or open source.
- Respect different opinions and experiences. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information (an address, an email, a server's credentials) without permission.
- Trolling, spam, or repeatedly derailing discussions.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every Laravelify space: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any channel where you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report behavior that breaks this code to **conduct@laravelify.cloud**. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone whose behavior breaks this code.
